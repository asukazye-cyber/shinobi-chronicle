# Development status

## Implemented (v0.2)

- React/TypeScript web foundation and data-driven content pack validation.
- Seeded deterministic RNG, versioned game-state codec, append-only chronicle, calendar and time-based injury recovery.
- Complete playable career loop: Academy → Genin → Chuunin → Jounin → retirement/fallen ending → Hall of Legends biography.
- Academy prologue: every new life begins with a short first-day scene involving the seeded mentor and rival. The player protects someone, traces a suspicious marker, or accepts the rival's challenge; that choice records a chronicle event and establishes a contextual support, perception, or mobility inclination without fixing a clan, family or future build.
- D–A mission ladder, evolving mission templates, seasonal world events, faction trust, honor/notoriety and lifetime statistics.
- Explainable combat report, finite chakra pool and technique costs, imperfect intelligence, preparation choices, withdrawal consequence, rewards, injuries, dōjutsu strain, mastery and rank-gated techniques.
- Persistent sensei/rival/teammate bonds, team-preparation threshold, Tracker specialization, Kurogane dōjutsu activation and named Toad contract bond.
- Persistent contact roster with availability, goals, memories and independent seasonal deployments; a selectable two-ally field team with cohesion; mission dilemmas requiring protect/pursue/negotiate priority before deployment.
- World-state layer with border tension, council trust, persistent rumors and discovered secrets; authored-style NPC arc moments whose choices change bonds, team cohesion, honor, council trust and secrets.
- Browser-local autosave/load for the active life and a persistent Hall of Legends archive of up to 30 completed biographies.
- Full Jinchūriki roster: Shukaku, Matatabi, Isobu, Son Gokū, Kokuō, Saiken, Chōmei, Gyūki, Kurama and Jūbi each use the same trust/respect/control/synchronization/unrest relationship layer but impose a different active field rule. Cooperation, suppression and domination remain meaningful paths; every cloak has a tactical identity rather than a generic damage boost. Jūbi is deliberately gated to a developed Jounin containment route.
- Dōjutsu study loop with insight, strain and three evolution stages; reciprocal summon-contract loop with bond and earned favor.
- Economy/logistics baseline: ryo-funded mission supplies and consumable sealing preparation. Ferimentos não exigem inventário, botões ou pesquisa: recuperam com o avanço natural dos dias.
- Anti-bureaucracy pass: one-click recommended mission preparation; detailed preparation remains optional behind a collapsed panel.
- Protagonist-focused modes: three distinct Sage disciplines (Stone, Storm, Veil), three trainable Gates, and an advanced Bijū mantle. Each has independent prerequisites, field effects, and explicit chakra/health/unrest costs. Summons can now intervene once in a fight through bond/favor rather than acting as a static bonus.
- Expanded build catalogue: 129 jutsu spanning all tactical tags, unusual utility/control techniques and prerequisite branches; eight specializations; ten dōjutsu (Kurogane, Lumen, Ashen, Sharingan, Byakugan, Rinnegan, Tenseigan, Ketsuryugan, Jōgan and Kagura); twelve mechanical Kekkei Genkai/styles (including Areia, Magnetismo, Lava, Fervura, Calor, Tempestade, Explosão and Metal); five elemental-nature trees; and five summon contracts. Each dōjutsu has an equipable technique, activation/strain progression, a costly combat focus and a distinct three-stage story arc. A dedicated UI catalogue enforces one strong affinity/lineage path per life.
- Modo Rikudō: exclusive to a stable Jūbi Jounin with hard Control/Sync/Respect, Willpower and Chakra Control gates. It adds a short high-cost mission transformation—broad tactical perception/control, large combat leverage, chakra/health cost, notoriety and unrest—then always dissipates after the operation.
- Replaced the old four-stat sheet with the main shinobi profile: Ninjutsu, Taijutsu, Genjutsu, Inteligência, Força, Velocidade, Stamina, Selos, Controle de Chakra and Força de Vontade. The UI displays raw score plus E–S+ band; old saves are migrated automatically.
- Progression-cap pass: raw training is bounded by career phase—Academy C (6), Genin B (8), Chuunin A (10), Jounin S (12). A prodigy’s single seeded signature (or a late-bloomer’s awakened signature) may exceed only that attribute’s phase ceiling by one grade: e.g. a Genin can reach A/9 in one specialty while all other stats stop at B/8. S+ is no longer farmable across the sheet and remains reserved for exceptional Jounin/legacy events. Existing saves are recalibrated to their current career ceiling on load, preventing an old Academy save from carrying endgame values forward.
- Development-choice pass: raw attribute growth is no longer a ten-button grind. At every career phase, the player chooses exactly two focuses before training, then has a small, visible number of decisive sessions (Academy 5, Genin 6, Chuunin 7, Jounin 8). Once the first session is spent, those priorities cannot be changed until promotion; missions instead grow jutsu mastery. Old saves retain their existing build but are marked as pre-choice saves and cannot use the new sessions retroactively.
- Build-reading pass: the UI now separates **Kit de campo** (only learned/equipped jutsu that participate in combat), **Biblioteca** (study and mastery), and **Escolhas permanentes** (doctrine, bloodline/Kekkei Genkai, summon and exceptional Bijū route). These channels explicitly do not compete for base jutsu slots, and optional catalogues are collapsed to avoid presenting the whole content pack as one confusing tree.
- Build-consequence pass: all eight specializations now have a situational field rule with a stated trade-off (for example, Vanguard gains an opening under pressure but begins more exposed; Shadow Runner gains safe angles in infiltration; Sealwright converts real seal preparation into boundaries). The build readout shows active tactical roles, actual synergies, missing answers/counters, and channel ownership instead of presenting a universal power score.
- Mod contract pass: `ContentPack` plus deterministic `mergeContentPacks` now support schema-matched, data-only additions of jutsu, specializations, bloodlines, dōjutsu, summons and Bijū. Conflicting ids and schema mismatches fail before content validation; packs cannot execute simulation code or mutate saves.
- Character creation now has controlled, seed-based variation: three aptitude points and one personality trade-off create distinct starts. Seeded potential gives roughly 10% of lives a **Prodigy** profile (one pronounced aptitude, one blind spot, and extra attention in B+ missions), roughly 15% a **Difficult Start** profile (lower early affinity but a +2 breakthrough aligned with the Academy choice after three successful missions), and the remainder a balanced ordinary profile. No profile locks a build or replaces player development; Neutral remains available for players who want no trait modifier.
- Origin and mentor pass: every seed now also produces a non-family Academy memory, latent tactical tag, and a named mentor with one of five doctrines. Mentors retain the sensei relationship and offer at most three bond-gated lessons, each granting a doctrine-specific attribute development plus optional contextual mission counsel; they do not create daily maintenance screens.
- Rival pass: every seed now generates an original named Blue-style career rival with a coherent style/affinity, preferred tactical response, independent reputation/rivalry, career-stage encounters and lasting memory. Challenge, study and earned alliance are limited between-operation choices; studying grants one counterpoint development per career stage instead of repeatable farming.
- Career depth pass: the Chuunin examination records an actual field priority; Jounin can earn one irreversible ANBU, Sensei, Commander or missing-nin path. Each path changes operational rules, S-rank crisis content and the final legacy. Kage is an earned Commander legacy, not a menu selection.
- Campaign-depth pass: three general S-rank crises and two S-rank operations for each Jounin appointment now join the mission pool. Builds can unlock three-stage personal operations (B → A → S) for affinity/lineage, summon contract, Bijuu bond or Sage practice; an advanced rival can culminate in its own S-rank resolution. Nine seeded seasonal events keep rumors, border tension, council trust, resources, natural chakra and reputation moving between operations. Completing an arc records a durable world secret, honor/reputation consequence, chronicle entry and save-safe story flag; failure leaves the operation unresolved instead of silently completing it.
- Dōjutsu expansion: Kurogane, Lumen and Ashen now have distinct B → A → S sight/responsibility arcs, gated by actual eye stage and insight rather than the generic affinity chain. The five summon contracts also use separate operation text and dilemmas consistent with their reconnaissance, tracking, concealment, terrain or route-reading identities.
- Career-crisis pass: failed missions now build a visible defeat streak instead of silently disappearing. Severe failures create injuries whose recovery is determined only by time; after a serious recovery, a contextual scar can change only relevant field commitments (pressure, ocular focus or transformation), never a flat permanent stat penalty. A prolonged collapse can create a council warrant and make the missing-nin route an earned escape/choice rather than a cosmetic job selection.
- Faction foundation: Pacto do Eclipse, Mãos Rubras, Diretoria de Caçadores and Lótus Cinzento each have agenda, standing and heat. B-rank-and-above briefings identify their involvement; mission result/priority and seasonal events alter their memory. A missing-nin is actively pursued by the Caçadores, while criminal networks and civilian dissidents react differently.
- Faction/Nukenin operations: relationships with the four factions now surface authored B → A-rank consequences instead of merely relabeling a procedural mission. The Eclipse moves from rare-power ownership to a living archive; Red Hands moves from illegal dependence to a refugee crossing; Ashen Lotus moves from a refuge to its evacuation. The chosen mission priority writes a durable faction-memory flag and changes political trust, honor, notoriety, underground ties and faction heat rather than a generic reputation number. A missing-nin must first survive the Hunter Directorate dossier and then resolve a public reckoning; only council trust and honor can unlock a return. Return clears the active hunt but preserves the chronicle and does not grant a replacement appointment for free.
- Genin now has a regional inter-village field circuit after two successful missions. Its three crisis responses establish a reusable Guardião, Batedor or Mediador signature, feed distinct social/world consequences, and influence later compatible career operations without locking the player out of a build.
- Tournament pass: promotion is no longer a button after thresholds. The Chuunin exam is a seeded three-round regional bracket at Vale das Pontes; Jounin is a seeded three-round international bracket at the Cúpula das Cinco Rotas. Each round names an opponent, accepts reading/control/commitment as a tactical answer, records wins and choices, and requires at least one win for promotion. A perfect combat build is not mandatory, but an unprepared build may leave the tournament without promotion and must develop through normal career play before returning.
- Focus-UI pass: career play defaults to a compact **journey** view showing development, kit, build reading and the active mission. Arsenal, permanent paths, modes, logistics, relationships, world and chronicle are available through **Abrir arsenal e mundo**, rather than demanding attention on every turn.
- Village-life pass: an optional collapsed routine offers four purposeful between-mission activities instead of a mandatory daily checklist. Practice advances the least-developed equipped technique; listening gathers up to two local leads that reduce the next mission's opening pressure; a mentor visit progresses lesson access; a two-day patrol earns modest ryo/honor/village trust and reduces border tension. Each advances calendar time and writes to the chronicle.
- Competitive-scene pass: every career has seeded Genin, Chuunin and Jounin rosters with age, years at rank, OVR, profile, form and record. The protagonist receives the same scouting OVR and component readout; it is a table/season metric, never a universal combat winner. Tournament opponents now come from the appropriate roster and expose their scouting line. At each completed in-game year, the circuit evolves and an annual report records player OVR movement, changed attributes, mastery, mission record, form headline and placement.
- Hybrid-build pass: a primary doctrine is now a starting method, not the end of a character. After two missions, a shinobi may invest time/ryo to cross-train up to two further disciplines; their jutsu branches become learnable alongside the primary branch. Explicit combinations (Sealwright + Shadow Runner, Field Medic + Mirage Weaver, Tracker + Ironhand, Tactician + Vanguard) create explainable field rules with advantages, pressure or exposure trade-offs. Bloodline remains biologically singular, while hybrid expression comes from technique, preparation and practice rather than fake multi-bloodline stacking.
- Fast-match combat pass: missions now resolve in one action, like a simulated sports fixture. The player still chooses priority, preparation, field plan and loadout beforehand; the engine then selects a coherent three-beat sequence from that kit and returns an explainable report of the decisive turns. There are no per-exchange buttons in a normal mission.
- Combat loadouts now behave as kits rather than isolated buttons: the simulator rotates lead techniques where possible and adds a compatible off-lead technique as a chakra-costing continuation. An active dōjutsu becomes an automatic costly opening commitment; a summon only joins when explicitly requested during preparation and its bond/favor condition is met.
- Slot architecture pass: base jutsu deck now grows 3 Genin → 4 Chuunin → 5 Jounin and is automatically completed with replaceable starter techniques on rank-up or old-save migration. A persistent jutsu library now separates study from equipment and preserves mastery off-deck. Dōjutsu, specialization, summon, Bijuu, personal modes and logistics remain visible independent channels instead of competing for those slots.
- First authored technique-tree slice: prerequisite-validated advanced jutsu branches (restraint, sentinel, mobility and sealwright) now require their parent techniques and, when relevant, specialization. First real dōjutsu combat technique layer: one lineage-specific focus per mission with chakra/strain cost and different field rules for Kurogane, Lumen and Ashen.
- Automated tests for validation, event publication/logging, determinism, save/load, progression, promotion/legacy, seasonal world simulation, risk/reward, and combat explanations.

## Designed, not implemented

Full NPC schedules and independent lives beyond seasonal availability; teams with tactics, conflict and member departure; more S-rank mission arcs; additional dōjutsu technique trees; summon encounters and contract requests; additional Bijū transformations/content; additional Sage/Gates forms; more Kekkei Genkai families and clan-specific authored arcs; multi-operation faction allegiances and multi-village politics beyond the current contract/heat model; longer authored narrative chains beyond the current three-stage personal operations; mentor turning-point scenes beyond the current doctrine/lesson model; crafting and broader economy/research; browser UI for importing and ordering validated mod packs; and save migrations beyond v1.

## Decisions

React + TypeScript + Vite was chosen because the empty repository needs a fast, portable UI shell while keeping the simulation independent and testable. No external images are required.
